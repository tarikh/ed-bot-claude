## What Ed Bot provides

Ed Bot helps founders, leaders and coaches explore leadership and working
relationships through Ed Batista's published writing. It combines coaching
instructions, bundled published articles and a read-only source service hosted
on ChatGPT Sites. Semantic editions find relevant passages by meaning and read
complete essays. Other editions may search the archive directly. The host writes
the answer; the source service returns published material.

The tools cannot edit sources, import arbitrary URLs or provide unpublished
material. Ed Batista is not personally responding. Using Ed Bot does not
establish a private or confidential coaching relationship with Ed or Tarikh.

## Use your judgment

Generated responses may be incomplete, inaccurate or unsuitable for your
circumstances. Check important claims against the linked source articles and
make your own decisions. Suggested conversation wording and reflections are
AI-generated assistance, not quotations unless clearly labeled.

## Access, cost and availability

Ed Bot has no payments, purchases or paid-access flow. Access to ChatGPT, Claude
and plugin features depends on the host's account, plan, workspace and availability
rules. A public semantic release is being prepared; these terms do not announce
its availability or guarantee approval.

Semantic search has a shared usage allowance and can be temporarily unavailable
when the allowance is reached or an upstream service fails. The current external-
search limits are 30 per minute, 500 per UTC day and 5,000 per UTC month across
all users. Exact-title/newest lookups and essay reads do not use that external
search allowance. The assistant should disclose unavailable retrieval, avoid
inventing sources and distinguish a bundled-source response from a successful
semantic search. Availability of bundled references depends on the host.
