Some day you will

I saw this plank nailed to a telephone pole in my neighborhood this afternoon, and I found its cryptic message both reassuring and daunting.  It offered encouragement, but it also challenged me to step up and make something happen.  It made me pause and think about all the things I'm not doing now but that I will do...some day.  What things?  And when?  And is some day... tomorrow?
