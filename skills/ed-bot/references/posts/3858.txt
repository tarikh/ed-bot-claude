CLASS 5: RESILIENCE

- The Resilience Factor (Karen Reivich and Andrew Shatté)

- Chapter 3, pages 48-62, “Laying the Groundwork”

- Bouncing Back: Rewiring Your Brain for Maximum Resilience and Well-Being (Linda Graham)
