1. Any power we have distorts what others choose to tell us and what we choose to hear.

2. The more power we have relative to someone else, the more distortion in the dialogue.

3. Any unpleasant truths we hear from someone with less power than we have are Precious Gifts, and should be treated accordingly.

4. All that said, feedback is not a gift.
