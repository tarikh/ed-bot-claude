An opportunity presents itself. It's intriguing, but you're not sure if you should take it.

So here are some questions to ask:

- What will this allow me to do that I can't do now?

- And what am I willing to give up in exchange?

- What will this prevent me from doing that I would miss?

- And what am I not willing to give up?
