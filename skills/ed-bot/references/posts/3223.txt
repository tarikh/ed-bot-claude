Keith Robinson at To-Done has a handy list of tips to boost your blogging productivity (or at least keep posting to a minimal level while getting real work done). My two favorites complement each other:

- Take advantage of creative highs. This is huge. There are times

when I’m literally flooded with ideas, when this happens I drop what

I’m doing, if possible, and write, write, write. During these times I

can build up a surplus of good content to use when I need some time

off. Which brings me to…

- Take some time off. There is

little better for fueling your creative engine than a few well timed

days off. As much as I create, there are many times when I just relax

and enjoy the fruits of my labor. I need this time to keep me going.

Thanks to Lifehacker.
