Here's the 2016 syllabus for The Art of Self-Coaching, the course I designed and taught for the first time last year at the Stanford Graduate School of Business. It's largely consistent with my 2015 syllabus, although there are some new readings, and I'll be making a few changes in how we work with the material in the classroom.

I'm eager to meet the next group of 36 students who'll be joining me on this journey--our first class is this Friday at 8am!

(And I'm cautiously optimistic that 2016 will also see the completion of my manuscript on this topic.)

As the class progresses I'll post my slides to this site's page for The Art of Self-Coaching course, where you can also find references to the course readings, other materials, and related posts of mine.
