I haven't been blogging much over the past week, thanks to a dead laptop, so when I took a look at my stats a few minutes ago, I was surprised to see a big jump. What gives?

It turns out that Tom Peters (yes, that Tom Peters) found his way here, liked what he saw, and encouraged his readers to drop by. And as Cathy Mosca notes, I've even been added to his blogroll. (I'm currently between Dave Barry and the esteemed Gary Becker & Richard Posner, which seems oddly fitting.)

I'm honored by the visits, and I hope you find a reason to come back. (I actually just upgraded my feed subscription options at the top of the left-hand sidebar, so if you like what you see, you can keep up with my posts from the comfort of your newsreader or email inbox.)
