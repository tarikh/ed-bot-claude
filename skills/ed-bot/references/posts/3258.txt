Kevin Aylward of Wizbang has a Standalone Trackback Pinger to allow people whose blogs don't support Trackback to create their own and send a Trackback to any blog that will accept them. I'm a TypePad loyalist for many reasons, including their support for Trackback, but it's nice to see tools like this that help extend the standard to other platforms that will (hopefully) get the message eventually.
