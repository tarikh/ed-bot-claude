I was in New York last week for Root Markets' Vaultstock, and among the many smart folks I met was Scott Matthews of Bitty Browser, an example of which you see above.  A Bitty Browser is a fully functional browser that sits within an existing web page--the one above is set to display Technorati's results for the "attentiontrust" tag.  Scott describes it as "picture-in-picture for the web."  Very cool.
