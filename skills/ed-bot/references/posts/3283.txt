The AttentionTrust gang will be convening at Web 2.0 tomorrow.  Seth and Steve will be hosting a session at 1:30pm, during which we'll be unveiling the alpha version of the AttentionTrust Extension, a Firefox extension that will allow users to begin to record aspects of their "attention data."  This is a small but significant step toward our goal of empowering users to exert greater control over their attention data so that they can make effective use of it.  And no disrespect to AT's original web designers, but we'll also be unveiling long-overdue new look for the AT website.  Long overdue.
