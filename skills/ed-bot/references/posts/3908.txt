@edbatista @StanfordBiz I looked at your syllabus online. I want to be in your course!

— Brené Brown (@BreneBrown) April 30, 2016

The work of Brené Brown has had a big influence on my approach to coaching and teaching (and my life), so this was an honor and a thrill. In addition to Brown's TED talks on vulnerability and shame, I particularly value her book Daring Greatly, which explores these topics and their implications for our personal and professional relationships. (Chapter 2, Debunking the Vulnerability Myths, is on my syllabus for The Art of Self-Coaching.) Many thanks, Brené!
